# CounterLab learner-pilot consent information

Consent version: 1

This repository copy is the standardized information sheet. Recruitment must provide the project lead's current contact channel alongside it; personal contact details are intentionally not stored in the public repository.

## Invitation and purpose

You are invited to try CounterLab, an educational prototype about machine-learning evaluation. The session tests whether the workflow is understandable and whether participants can complete two fixed transfer tasks. It is not an exam, credential, employment assessment, or proof of mastery.

## What you will do

The session takes about 20–30 minutes. You will:

- review two synthetic notebook cases;
- state and commit predictions before seeing computed results;
- inspect verified experiment evidence;
- record a revision; and
- answer one fixed transfer task for each concept.

The order of the two concepts is randomized. The facilitator may help with navigation or accessibility but will not give answers.

## Data collected

The study export records a random participant ID, randomization slot, consent and task timestamps, completion, transfer pass/fail, whether a prediction changed, and task duration.

The study export does not include your name, email, IP address, raw claim or revision prose, notebook uploads, or private model reasoning. Recruitment contact details and any signed consent record are kept separately from the pseudonymous task export.

## Risks and benefits

Risks are expected to be minor: temporary frustration, fatigue, or discomfort from making an incorrect prediction. You may pause, skip a task, or stop. There is no guaranteed personal benefit, though you may find the evaluation examples useful.

## Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Participation is voluntary. Declining or stopping has no penalty and does not affect grades, employment, services, or access to CounterLab. You may request deletion while the facilitator can still connect your withdrawal code to the pseudonymous record. After de-identification and aggregation, individual deletion may no longer be possible.

## Use and reporting

The team will use structured records to improve the prototype and report aggregate descriptive results. The pilot has no untreated control group, and reports will not claim causality or mastery. No identifiable quote will be published from this data format.

## Consent statements

Before starting, the facilitator must record affirmative agreement to all statements:

1. I am at least 18 years old.
2. I have read this information and had an opportunity to ask questions.
3. I understand participation is voluntary and I may stop without penalty.
4. I understand what structured data is collected and what is excluded.
5. I consent to participate under consent version 1.

If any statement is not affirmed, do not create a pilot session record.
