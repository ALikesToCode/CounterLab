## Scoring integrity

- Unreachable, disabled, replay-only, undocumented, or unbound code receives limited credit.
- Software tests do not count as learner-impact data.
- Readiness/configuration health does not count as a completed production journey.
- “Distinctive synthesis” is supported; categorical uniqueness is not.
- The public app was available during the audit. This score does not allege an outage.
