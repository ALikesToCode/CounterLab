## Class-imbalance anti-template proof

prevalence). The computed majority baseline reaches 0.989333 accuracy while its
rare-class recall is 0. The stratified model's PR-AUC is 0.196393, and lowering

including inconsistent confusion totals, a literal majority baseline, fake
PR-AUC, an inert threshold, prevalence drift, and nondeterministic hashes.

identification of the centered rolling target as future leakage. Failure keeps
the patch locked. The public patch changes cell 3, preserves four unrelated cell
source hashes, uses whole-customer grouping, removes customer identity, produces
